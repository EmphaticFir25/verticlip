Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Verticlip ("Verticlip," "we," "us") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. Verticlip is operated by Verticlip. Questions about this policy can be sent to jthomaselder6@gmail.com.

1. Information we collect

Account information from TikTok. When you connect a TikTok account with Login Kit, we receive your TikTok open ID and display name. We request the user.info.basic and video.upload permissions. We use them only to identify your account in Verticlip and to send clips you choose to your TikTok inbox.

Account information from YouTube (Google). When you connect a YouTube channel, we receive your channel ID and channel title. We request the youtube.upload and youtube.readonly permissions. We use them only to identify your channel and to upload Shorts you choose to publish.

Content you provide. This includes video URLs you paste, logo images you upload for watermarks, and the clips generated from those videos.

Technical data. Standard server logs, such as request times and error messages, which we use to keep the service running.

2. How we use information

  • To generate clips, captions, and watermarked videos from the content you choose.
  • To upload selected clips to the YouTube and TikTok accounts you connected.
  • To operate, secure, and troubleshoot the service.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use TikTok or YouTube user data for advertising. We do not use TikTok user data to train AI models.

3. Processing and storage

Video transcription and clip scoring run on the server where Verticlip is hosted, using a local AI model. Transcripts are used to find clip moments and are not shared with third parties. OAuth tokens for connected accounts are stored on the server so that uploads can run without asking you to sign in again. Generated clips are stored until you delete them or remove the account.

4. Sharing

We share information only with the platforms you connect to, to carry out the uploads you request: Google (YouTube) and TikTok. We may also disclose information if required by law.

5. Your choices and deletion

  • Disconnect an account inside Verticlip to remove its stored tokens.
  • Revoke access at any time from your Google account permissions page or your TikTok app settings.
  • Request deletion of your data by emailing jthomaselder6@gmail.com. We will delete your stored tokens, uploaded logos, and generated clips within 30 days.

6. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect stored information, but no system is completely secure. You are responsible for keeping your devices and accounts secure.

7. Children

Verticlip is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. Accounts connected to YouTube or TikTok must belong to users who meet those platforms' age requirements.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it last changed. Material changes will be announced in the app.
